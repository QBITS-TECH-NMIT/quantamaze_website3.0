About the Event

Quant-A-Maze 3.0 is the flagship 36-hour national-level hackathon organized by Q-Bits, the official Quantum Technology Club of Nitte Meenakshi Institute of Technology (NMIT), anchored within the Department of Electrical & Electronics Engineering.

Q-Bits partners closely with KwantumG Research Labs Pvt. Ltd., an NMIT-incubated deep-tech R&D lab led by Founder & CEO Karthiganesh Durai, giving participants access to industry-grade quantum computing, mentorship, and applied research pathways.

Building on last year's edition — which drew 500+ participants from 50+ top colleges across 10+ states — Quant-A-Maze 3.0 brings students together to build across Quantum Machine Learning, Post-Quantum Cryptography, Web3 & Blockchain, and Generative AI & ML.

Quantum Machine Learning (QML)

TRACK_02
◆

Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC)

Secure tomorrow before quantum arrives. Design quantum-resistant encryption, digital signatures, and secure communication protocols using emerging post-quantum cryptographic standards.

Web3 & Blockchain

Build the internet of trust. Develop smart contracts, dApps, DeFi platforms, decentralized identity systems, and NFTs using Ethereum, Solidity, Polygon, and IPFS.

TRACK_04

Generative AI & ML

Build the intelligence of tomorrow. Develop AI copilots, autonomous agents, predictive models, and NLP applications using LLMs, TensorFlow, PyTorch, Hugging Face, and LangChain.
